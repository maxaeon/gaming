Peer Review Workshop

Welcome to the Philosophy Peer Review Workshop! You'll practice giving helpful feedback on a short essay about Kant's ethics.

Peer Review Example

Kant argues that moral rules must hold without exception. Critics reply that this rigidity ignores cases where following a rule creates obvious harm. Consider Kant’s claim that lying is always wrong even if it helps protect an innocent person.





    Peer Review Practice

    Thesis Statement: Kant’s ethics is too rigid because it demands rules without exceptions.

    Kant thinks you should always tell the truth. But what if a murderer is at your door? Then telling the truth would get someone killed. This example highlights how strict adherence to Kant’s rule clashes with common-sense morality. Many philosophers argue that moral decisions need flexibility to prevent obvious harm.

    Imagine a friend hides in your house to escape danger. If you follow Kant strictly, you must reveal their location when questioned. Critics say this shows why any moral system needs room for exceptions.
