Peer Review Workshop

Welcome to the Philosophy Peer Review Workshop! You'll practice giving helpful feedback on a short essay about Kant's ethics.

Peer Review Example

Kant says morality has rules, but sometimes rules are too strict, and that can be bad.


Peer Review Practice

Thesis Statement: Kant’s ethics is too rigid because it demands rules without exceptions.

Kant thinks you should always tell the truth. But what if a murderer is at your door? Then telling the truth would get someone killed. This is why Kant’s rules don’t always work.
