Citation Activity

Accurate citations help readers locate your sources, strengthen your credibility, and prevent plagiarism. Philosophical writing relies heavily on accurate referencing. This activity practices MLA in-text and Works Cited formatting. Other citation styles may be preferred in different contexts, so always follow your assignment guidelines.
