Citation Guide

Consult this quick reference after completing the Citation Investigator.

In-Text Citation Basics

Include the author's last name and page number in parentheses: (Kant 31).

When the author's name appears in the sentence, cite only the page: Kant writes… (31).

For sources without page numbers, use a section or paragraph number if available.

Works Cited Entries

Arrange your sources alphabetically by the author's last name. Common formats include:

  • Book: Last, First. Title. Publisher, Year.
  • Chapter in Edited Book: Last, First. "Chapter Title." Book Title, edited by Editor, Publisher, Year, pp. #–#.
  • Journal Article: Last, First. "Article Title." Journal, vol. #, no. #, Year, pp. #–#.
  • Website: Last, First. "Title of Page." Website Name, Day Mon. Year, URL.

Quoting and Paraphrasing

Introduce quotations with context and cite the source. Paraphrased ideas also require citations.

Additional Resources
